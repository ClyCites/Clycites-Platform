# Pilot training and legal review checklist

## Field and support training

Each named participant completes role-specific exercises on representative devices:

- Collection agents: sign-in, organization confirmation, device/session checks, snapshot age,
  offline delivery capture, conflict handling, sync confirmation, correction requests, receipt
  reprint, logout, and lost-device escalation.
- Cooperative administrators: membership/device revocation, correction review, lot and custody
  checks, incident creation, privacy-request assignment, and readiness evidence review.
- Finance officers: proceeds verification, settlement exceptions, separation of duties, masked
  payment destinations, manual/mock instruction submission, reconciliation, and dispute escalation.
- Support operators: privacy-safe diagnostics, queue backlog interpretation, feature-flag rollback,
  backup evidence, incident severity, and prohibited secret/PII handling.

Record participant, role, trainer, date, equipment, scenarios completed, unresolved gaps, and human
reviewer. Seed fixtures and automated tests are not training completion.

## Uganda legal and regulatory review

Qualified local counsel or the designated accountable reviewer must assess, at minimum:

- lawful basis, notice, consent, correction, withdrawal, access, deletion, restriction, retention,
  legal holds, processor roles, cross-border processing, and incident reporting;
- farmer and cooperative terms, marketplace disclosures, electronic records/signatures, payment
  instructions, disputes, taxes, and record-keeping obligations;
- SMS consent, sender identity, opt-out, template content, provider terms, and delivery records;
- Hedera testnet disclosure, public metadata, privacy-safe hashes, key custody, and the prohibition
  on representing consensus as proof of real-world truth;
- field device custody, scale calibration, receipt equipment, accessibility, language accuracy, and
  support escalation.

The application records review evidence but does not make legal conclusions. Keep the legal gate
blocked until the accountable human reviewer records approval or a documented risk decision.
