# Phase 5: Cooperative lot marketplace

Phase 5 lets a cooperative publish quality-approved lot quantity to verified buyer organizations.
Buyers can discover eligible listings, submit exact quantity and minor-unit price offers, negotiate
counter-offers, withdraw an offer, and reject terms addressed to them. Seller acceptance atomically
reserves inventory and creates a sales contract for approval by both organizations.

An active contract can produce one order. The seller attaches the matching custody transfer, and the
order follows physical dispatch and receipt. The buyer records a completed inspection and then fully
accepts, partially accepts, or rejects the received quantity. Full acceptance allows the seller to
complete the order and contract. Exceptions remain explicit as rejected or disputed commercial
states rather than being represented as payment failures.

Cooperatives can privately share selected traceability scopes with a named buyer and revoke access at
any time. Expiry automatically closes stale listings, offers, invitations, reservations, and shares.
Phase 5 records commercial terms and fulfillment only. It does not move money or implement farmer
settlement, mobile money, escrow, stablecoins, HTS, lending, insurance, or tokenized ownership.
