# Product scope

The initial product direction covers cooperative registration of farmers and farms, offline coffee
delivery and quality capture, traceable lot creation, buyer sales, transparent farmer settlement,
payment reconciliation, and selected Hedera event anchoring.

Phase 1 implements staff authentication, users, organizations and memberships, collection points,
farmers, farms, consent history, audit records, and opaque farmer QR identities. Platform
administrators create organizations. Cooperative staff work only within active memberships and
explicit role permissions. Authorized staff may resolve an active QR identifier to a deliberately
limited farmer profile.

Phase 1 deliberately excludes deliveries, batches, lots, settlements, payments, marketplace
workflows, offline mutation synchronization, cryptocurrency, tokens, smart contracts, and real
Hedera transactions.

Success means a cooperative can be onboarded, staff access can be governed, and farmer identity
records can be collected and resolved without crossing organization boundaries or exposing PII in
QR payloads.
